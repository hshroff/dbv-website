Join us

We’d love to welcome you to the DBV family.

Register your child, sign up as a volunteer, or reach out with any questions. We’re a community that grows every year, and there’s a seat for you here, whether you grew up with Indian culture or are discovering it for the first time.

Ways to get involved

There’s a place for every family.

01

Register for classes

Registration for the upcoming year is open now. Classes run every Sunday, September through May, from 2:20–4:30 PM for children pre-K (age 3) through grade 12.

Register now
02

Join the Youth Group

Grade 7 and above? Put cultural values into action through service projects, guest speakers, debate, and mentorship, and build friendships for life.

Explore DBVYG
03

Volunteer with us

Teachers, mentors, and committee members are the heart of DBV. Whatever your time looks like, we’d love to have you help shape the next generation.

Getting started

Your first Sunday in four easy steps

1

Register

Complete the registration form and we’ll reach out to confirm your spot.

2

Get assigned

We’ll assign your child to the right culture and language class for their age.

3

Come on Sunday

Arrive at E.N. Peirce Middle School by 2:20 PM and meet your child’s teacher.

4

Become family

Connect with other families, join events, and watch your child grow rooted.
