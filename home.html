Who we are

A community built by volunteers, for the next generation.

Downingtown Balvihar (DBV) is a close-knit, volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit in the West Chester, PA area, built by parents who want our kids to grow up knowing who they are and where they come from. Through classes and hands-on experience, we share Indian culture, values, languages, traditions, festivals, and history.

We gather every Sunday from September through May, with classes for children age 3 (pre-K) through grade 12. You don’t need to know a thing about Indian culture to join us.
