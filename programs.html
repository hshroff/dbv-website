Culture class overview

Understanding who we are, and where we come from.

“Who are we, and where do we come from?” That’s the question at the heart of our culture classes. Students start young with interactive, story-based learning, then move on to deeper topics as they grow.

The vision of the culture class is to provide an overview of India’s values, food, music, arts, festivals, history, and geography, plus discussions on social topics and individual contributions to today’s world. Our teachers approach every child like their own: kind, patient, and full of encouragement.

We emphasize integrating Indian values with American culture, fostering children to become good and responsible role models for the next generation.

DBV Youth Group

Where cultural identity turns into action.

For DBV graduates who are motivated to explore, develop, and integrate cultural relevance into their modern lives, the Youth Group puts cultural values into motion. Under the guidance of dedicated mentors, members volunteer, teach, discuss, and lead.
