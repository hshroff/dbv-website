Events & News

We host several cultural events throughout the year to bring families together, express creativity, tell stories, entertain ourselves, and fly high for the future.

Community Service · February

Working with People’s Pantry

During the month of February, DBV children participated in the Youth Group’s “Spread the Love” campaign. They earned jars of peanut butter and jelly to donate by doing a good deed, collecting 60 jars in total to benefit the People’s Pantry!

Youth Group · Saturday Service

Project C.U.R.E. Service Project

Youth Group students volunteered their Saturday morning to help Project C.U.R.E. send much-needed medical supplies to people in need across the world.
