A key figure in the establishment of Downingtown Balvihar is Mr. Vilas Desai (pictured here with his wife Lata Desai).

Lovingly known as Vilas Uncle, his vision, persistence, and nonstop community effort, supported by his daughter Sujata and son-in-law Ashish Shah, led to the official establishment of Downingtown Balvihar in July 2007. He poured his heart and soul into this endeavor so that our children get to know about our great culture and rich languages.

Classes started with just 15 children in Bell Tavern Park. By the end of that year, the organization was meeting in school classrooms, and by 2011, well over 150 children were attending classes every Sunday.

Vilas Uncle still stops by to visit during events and is an enduring inspiration to all of us.
