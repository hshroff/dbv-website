Our story

A community built by volunteers, for the next generation.

Downingtown Balvihar is a volunteer-driven, IRS-certified 501(c)(3) nonprofit that gives school-aged children classroom and experience-based learning of Indian culture, and a community to call their own.

 

Mission

To provide children with instructional as well as experience-based learning of Indian culture, values, languages, traditions, festivals, and our history, along with kindness and leadership skills, to foster their development as capable global citizens.

Vision

To promote, instill, and foster Indian culture in the children of our community, building character, self-esteem, and leadership to raise them as responsible and compassionate citizens.

Through weekly classes and special events, children build a stronger connection to their heritage and develop a self-confident, lasting identity as Indian Americans. Most of all, we’re neighbors and friends who’ve chosen to raise our children together.

Board & leadership

Oversight and guidance from people who serve.

The board of Downingtown Balvihar provides oversight and guidance for the organization. It is made up of members who have served in a number of different volunteer and leadership roles, and who keep our mission at the center of every decision.

If you’re thinking about getting involved, come say hello. There are no strangers at DBV, only families we haven’t met yet.

Our teachers & mentors

Teachers are the pillars of Downingtown Balvihar, committed volunteers dedicated to classroom instruction that helps our students learn and grow. They work with both the executive team and parents to provide the best experience for every student.

Each teacher who has devoted time and energy to Balvihar has done so without asking for anything in return. Despite numerous personal responsibilities, they serve with grace and dedication, selflessly, every Sunday.

If you’re thinking of joining us, come say hello on a Sunday. Our teachers are friendly, patient, and happy to chat during sign-in.
